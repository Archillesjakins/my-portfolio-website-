Updated Proposal Strategy — Per Upwork Feedback
Target: AI-aware clients only
Look for job posts that use words like "automation", "AI agent", "workflow", "n8n", "ChatGPT integration", "RAG", "LLM". These clients already understand the value — you don't have to educate them. Skip generic "Python developer needed" posts unless the budget is strong and the problem is clear.
Connect to their workflow, not your stack
Instead of: "I have experience with LangChain, FastAPI, and multi-agent systems."

Write: "Your team is manually copying form data into your CRM every time a lead comes in. I'll build an agent that does that automatically the moment the form is submitted — no human touch required."

Same skills. The second version is what the client actually buys.
Proof order in every proposal
1. Their specific problem named in line 1
2. How you solve it (one sentence)
3. One proof point (Sono Health or UmukoziHR metric)
4. What you'd do for them specifically
5. CTA — one specific question or Zoom ask

Never list your stack before naming their problem.
